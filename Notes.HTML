    Q. Solve this riddle: I am an atom with mass number 23 and 11 protons. I am a soft metal and react vigrously with water. Who am I and how many nutreous do I have?

    Ans.

    The atom is sodium NA because it is soft and reacts vigourously with water.

    Mass number = Protons + Neutrons

    So,Neutrons = mass number - Protons

    Neutrons = 23 - 11 = 12

    Thus,the element is sodium NA and it has 12 Neutrons


    8.8 Combinning Capicity of an Atom: Valancy

    • The numbers of atoms of hydrogen or chlorine with which one atom of an element can combine to form a compound is called its combining capicity.
    • It is expressed in terms of hydrogen and clorine because both posses a combining capicity of one.
    • For example, in H2O (water), oxygen combines with two hydrogen atoms, so the combining caapicity of oxygen is two.
    • The outermost electron shell of an atom is called the Valence shell,and the electrone in it are called Valence electrons.
    • An atom with 8 electrone in its valence shell has an octet(helium is stable with 2 electrons).
    • Atoms with a complete valence shell are generally stable and unreactive.
    • Atoms with incomplete valence shells are more reactive and tend to lose,gain, or share electrons to complete their octet.

    Valency

    • The number of electrons gained,lost, or shared to complete the octet is called the valency of the element.
    • The valency of an elements depands on the number of electrons in its outermost shell.Atoms try to complete their octet to become stable.

      • How to Determine Valency

    • If the element has fewar than four electrone in its valency shell, it tends to lose electrone to complete its octet and become stable.
    • On the other hand, if the number of valence electrone is more then for, it tends to gain electrone to complete its octet.

    8.9 A Deeper Look into Atomic Structure

    Isotopes

    • Atoms of the same element having the same atomic number but different mass numbers are called isotopes.

      • Properties of Isotopes

    • Isotopes of an element have similar cheemical properties because they contain the same number of electrone and have the same electronic configration. However, their physical properties differ due to difference in mass.

      • Isotopes of Hydrogen

    • Hydrogen has three isotopes --- protium (1⁄2H)
      • , deuterium (2⁄1H),and tritium (1⁄3H).All three isotopes contain one proton and one electron, but they differ in the number of neutrons.Protium has no nuetron, deuterium has one neutron, and tritium has two neutrons.
